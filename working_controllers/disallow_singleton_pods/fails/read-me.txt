## Singleton Pod Blocker Admission Webhook

This webhook **blocks direct Pod creation**. Use Deployments, not bare Pods!

### Prerequisites

- cert-manager must be installed in your cluster.
  - [cert-manager install guide](https://cert-manager.io/docs/installation/)

### Installation

```sh
kubectl apply -f singleton-pod-blocker.yaml
kubectl -n webhook-demo wait --for=condition=complete job/webhook-certgen --timeout=60s
